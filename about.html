Professional Profile

I recently graduated with a Law degree from Universitas Andalas, majoring in Constitutional Law with a 3.72 GPA. Throughout my studies, I focused on public policy analysis, local regulations, and their impact on constitutional rights. I kept a healthy balance between academics and campus life, staying active in organizations like BEM and ALSA.

These roles gave me hands-on experience in analyzing criminal case files, reviewing fresh regulations, drafting business contracts, and assisting senior lawyers in client consultations. On top of that, working as a freelance document reviewer sharpened my attention to detail and ability to deliver polished work under tight deadlines.
